Welcome to Applied Calculus I! Over the next fourteen weeks we will work through a great deal of material, all of it aimed at equipping you with the tools of calculus. Use these notes to guide you through the course. Keep them with you, be diligent in your work, and you will be happy with your results at the end.

How to use these notes: these notes are different from most mathematics notes. They were written with gaps in them, and those gaps are now answer boxes for you to fill in. They are missing information on purpose! Rather than copying everything down, you only need to work out the gaps. If you need to add notes of your own to help you understand, use the margins. These are YOUR notes. Use them wisely.

It is worth glancing over a section before you work through it --- two to five minutes is plenty --- so that you know where it is heading.

Each section lists the corresponding sections of a standard single-variable calculus textbook, so that you can read alongside whichever book you are using.

Good luck. You can do this.
