\title{Derivatives of inverse trig functions}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We use implicit differentiation to find the derivative of $\arcsin(x)$ and record the derivatives of all six inverse trig functions. Textbook §3.5.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

We can use implicit differentiation in order to find the derivatives of inverse trig functions. Recall that $\arcsin (x)=\sin ^{-1}(x)=y$ means that $\sin (y)=x$ when $\frac{-\pi}{2} \leq y \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$. So let's test it out.

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d}{d x} \sin (y) & =\frac{d}{d x} x \\
\frac{d}{d y} \sin (y) \frac{d y}{d x} & =1 \\
\cos (y) \frac{d y}{d x} & =1 \\
\frac{d y}{d x} & =\frac{1}{\cos (y)}
\end{aligned}
\]

Since $\frac{-\pi}{2} \leq y \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$ then $\cos (y) \geq 0$. And since $\sin ^{2}(y)+\cos ^{2}(y)=1$ we have

\[
\cos (y)=\sqrt{1-\sin ^{2}(y)}=\answer{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}.
\]

In other words
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\arcsin(x)=\answer{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}}.
\]

Using similar approaches, we can find the derivatives for every inverse trig function:

\[
\begin{array}{l|l||l|l}
\arcsin (x) & \answer{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}} & \arccsc(x) & \answer{-\frac{1}{x \sqrt{x^{2}-1}}} \\
\arccos (x) & \answer{-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}} & \arcsec(x) & \answer{\frac{1}{x \sqrt{x^{2}-1}}} \\
\arctan (x) & \answer{\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}} & \arccot(x) & \answer{-\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}}
\end{array}
\]
